# AI Governance for CAPA

This document establishes the guardrails and guidelines for AI integration within the CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) workflow.

## Role of AI in CAPA
AI acts strictly as an **advisory tool** within the CAPA module. Its primary function is to assist human operators by analyzing identified gaps and suggesting potential paths forward.

### Allowed AI Capabilities
AI may be used to:
1.  **Suggest Gap Explanations:** Analyze missing, partial, or rejected evidence to draft a plain-language summary of the gap.
2.  **Suggest Root Causes:** Based on the gap description and framework context, draft potential root causes for the deficiency.
3.  **Suggest Corrective Actions:** Propose immediate steps to fix the identified issue.
4.  **Suggest Preventive Actions:** Recommend long-term procedural changes to prevent recurrence.
5.  **Draft CAPA Summaries:** Compile the above elements into a cohesive CAPA draft for human review.
6.  **Recommend Roles/Urgency:** Suggest an appropriate responsible role or severity level based on the indicator's context.

### Strict AI Prohibitions
To maintain governance integrity, AI is strictly prohibited from:
1.  **Approving or Closing a CAPA:** Only authorized human roles (Reviewers, Approvers) can close a CAPA.
2.  **Creating a Closed CAPA:** Any CAPA generated by AI must be created in a draft/open state.
3.  **Approving Evidence:** Linking closure evidence via AI does not auto-approve the evidence.
4.  **Modifying Readiness Status:** AI cannot manually override project readiness scores or export eligibility.

## Implementation Details
In the current MVP:
- AI acts only as an advisory generator for text fields (root cause, corrective action, preventive action).
- The `status` field transitions (e.g., from `IN_PROGRESS` to `CLOSED`) are programmatically restricted to human-initiated API calls via the `CAPAActionView`.
- The `close_capa` service in `backend/apps/indicators/capa_services.py` explicitly requires human actor verification and role authorization (`ensure_project_approver_access`).

These guardrails ensure that while AI accelerates the drafting process, all accreditation decisions remain human-governed.
