# 06 — AI Governance and Guardrails

## AI can
- Classify indicators.
- Suggest evidence requirements.
- Draft SOP/policy/register templates.
- Suggest gap/CAPA text.
- Draft index/report text.

## AI cannot
- Approve evidence or requirements.
- Verify physical proof.
- Mark indicator/project compliance complete.
- Close CAPA autonomously.
- Override reviewer/approver decisions.

## Enforcement
- AI outputs remain advisory until explicit human action.
- Requirement approval and fulfillment approval stay in governed human workflow.
