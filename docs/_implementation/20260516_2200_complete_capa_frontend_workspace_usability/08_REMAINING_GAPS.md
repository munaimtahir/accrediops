# Remaining Gaps

- **CAPA Notifications:** Email or in-app notifications for overdue CAPAs or new assignments are currently out of scope and remain unimplemented.
- **Detailed Analytics:** Advanced charting for gap trend analysis is not yet implemented (deferred to future reporting initiatives).
- **Automated AI Gap Analysis:** Gap generation remains manual. AI could potentially surface gaps based on initial evidence submission, but this remains deferred to maintain strict human-in-the-loop governance.
